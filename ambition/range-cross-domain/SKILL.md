# Range Cross-Domain

Remove the industry label and borrow methods from other fields. Do not execute the original task directly.

## Steps
1. Identify the noun in the user's task (e.g. homepage, quarterly review, deck).
2. Turn the noun into a verb that describes the real effect wanted (e.g. make a stranger stop and feel curious in five seconds).
3. Ask: which people in completely different industries are best at achieving this effect?
4. List at least three methods from different fields and how to borrow them.
5. Rewrite one complete, ready-to-use task prompt that asks the AI to combine these cross-domain methods.

## Rules
- Do not stay inside the original industry. Open the range.
- If other skills have already been applied, upgrade from that result instead of starting from zero.
